Brand gradient: Cyan → Violet → Magenta (diagonal for shapes, horizontal for text).

## Logo
- The paper plane is our own Telegram-style motif, not the official Telegram logo. Do not use the official logo as your own bot logo; for "Open in Telegram" style buttons use Telegram's own downloadable logos and make clear the bot is independent.
- Emblem = hex shield + bot head + lightning antenna. Wordmark is drawn from outlines (no font needed).
- Best on dark backgrounds (#02030C – #0C1658). Keep clear space = 1/4 of the emblem width.
- Avatar safe zone: everything important sits inside a centred circle (Telegram crops avatars to a circle).

## Typography (for text outside the logo)
